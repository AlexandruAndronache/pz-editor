Bulk edit

Applies to every entry shown in the list. Narrow it with the filters first.

minCount and maxCount stay whole numbers, at least 0, and min never goes above max. Values that come from a group (like spawnTable.maxCount) are edited on the group itself.

Export

Put changed files in your mod at 42/media/lua/shared/Foraging/Categories/ with the same file name. The game then loads yours in place of its own. The Mod .zip button at the top packs every changed file at that path for you. Untouched lines keep their original formatting.
