How it works

Pick an editor

Each one comes preloaded with its script file. You can also load your own modded .txt with “Open .txt…”.

Edit or bulk-edit

Filter the list, then change one entry or everything shown, like multiplying Weight or halving recipe time. Undo covers the last action.

Download and mod

Download the edited .txt (recipes: every changed file as one zip) and drop it into your mod at the same path as the original. Unsaved edits are kept in this browser between visits.
